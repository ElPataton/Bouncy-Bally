#GameModes #Challenges #Mutators #Progression #NewConcepts

Replayability systems, run mutators, board bosses, and anti-repetition mechanics designed to make every [[Prestige|Ascension]] run feel distinct from the last. These concepts complement standard `$2,500` runs and the existing `16` Goals.

---

# 1. Run Mutators ("Board Anomalies")

Players can toggle **1–2 Anomalies** before starting a run (or encounter them in Challenge Modes) to twist physics and economy in exchange for bonus **Cores** and **Prestige Shards**.

| Anomaly | Physics / Rule Twist | Strategic Shift | Reward Bonus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Zero-G Drift** | Gravity `-45%`, bounce elasticity `+35%` | Balls float and ricochet upward; `Basket` and `Zigzag` pins dominate the upper rows. | `+25%` Shards |
| **Fragile Pegs** | Pins break after `10` hits per wave, dropping a `$` burst and vanishing for `10s` | Opens sudden vertical chutes mid-wave; rewards fast `Ghost`/`Marble` bursts before lanes collapse. | `+30%` Shards |
| **Reverse Gravity Flip** | Every `5th` batch inverts gravity for `10s`, launching balls upward from the bottom | Ceiling multiplier buckets appear temporarily; `Pin Tweaker` biases invert cleanly. | `+35%` Shards |
| **One-Type Purist** | Only `1` configurable ball type allowed in all Batch Slots, but its special effect triggers `2×` faster | Turns `Chemical` (every `2` contacts) or `Sticky` (up to `5` contacts) into hyper-focused solo builds. | `+20%` Shards |
| **Taxman's Toll** | Center slots (`4–6`) pay `×0.2`, while extreme edge slots (`1` & `9`) pay `×5.0` | Forces aggressive `Pin Tweaker` (`30%`/`70%`) and [[Plans]] Wall sculpting to fight the bell curve. | `+40%` Shards |

> [!tip] Risk-Reward Stacking
> Anomalies never lock out core [[Skill tree]] progression—instead, they invert which upgrades are "good," making previously overlooked balls like `Beach` or `Basket` suddenly MVP.

# 2. Board Bosses & Milestone Encounters

Instead of a purely passive climb to `$2,500`, optional **Gatekeeper Bosses** spawn directly *on* the Galton board at `$500` milestones:

### The Magnetron (Row 3–4 Core)
- **Mechanic**: A hovering magnetic core pulls `Steel` and `Basic` balls toward the low-value center slot.
- **Counterplay**: Hit its `2` orbiting shield pins `15` times—or bypass its pull with non-magnetic `Ghost` / `Beach` balls—to shatter it.
- **Reward**: Drops a **Core Cache** (`+1` Core) and triggers a `10s` `×3` Golden Frenzy.

### The Greedy Mimic Slot
- **Mechanic**: Temporarily replaces one of your `Double slots` with a chomping Mimic that swallows balls for `$0` payout.
- **Counterplay**: Feed it `10` balls (or hit it once with a `Gambler` or `Golden` ball) to overfill it.
- **Reward**: Bursts open and pays out **2× the total value** of all swallowed balls at once.

### The Entropy Storm
- **Mechanic**: Spawns `3` drifting dampener clouds that slow balls by `50%` on contact.
- **Counterplay**: Clear clouds by blasting through them with `Turbo`-charged pins or high-arc `Basket` bounces.
- **Reward**: Clearing all `3` clouds within `30s` awards a free **Tinker** or **Charged** [[Lootboxes upgrades|Lootbox Capsule]] roll.

# 3. Dedicated Game Modes

### Daily Seeded Galton ("The Daily Board")
- **Format**: Every player gets the exact same pre-tweaked pin layout, `2` locked Pin Modifiers, `1` active Anomaly, and a **10-Batch Score Attack** limit (no infinite idle).
- **Goal**: Maximize total `$` in `10` batches to earn **Bronze / Silver / Gold / Prismatic** medal tiers and bonus Cores.

### Puzzle Draft ("1-Batch Brainteaser")
- **Format**: Hand-crafted or seeded mini-boards where you receive a fixed `$100` budget to place `3` Pin Modifiers and set `4` Pin Tweaker biases before pressing **Launch Once**.
- **Goal**: Hit a strict target payout in a **single batch**—pure spatial and synergy problem-solving with zero grinding.

### Endless Overdrive (Post-Ascension Mode)
- **Format**: Upon reaching `$2,500`, players can choose to **Ascend** immediately or toggle **Overdrive**.
- **Loop**: Every `30s`, ball speed and pin multipliers ramp up while board "Heat" rises. Players cash out at any time for bonus **Prestige Shards**—or push too far and trigger a **Meltdown** that ends the run with only base Shards.

> [!example] Overdrive Cash-Out Tension
> At `2.4×` Heat, your `Tesla` + `Turbo` setup is printing `$800/sec`, but balls are bouncing off-screen. Do you bank the `+3` extra Shards now, or risk `15` more seconds for `+5`?

# 4. Anti-Repetition Mechanics

### Drafted Ascension Relics
At the start of every [[Prestige|Prestige]] run, draft **1 of 3** run-altering Relics so Run `#12` plays differently from Run `#2`:
- *"Every `10th` launched ball is a guaranteed `randomOnly` ball (`Golden`, `Constructor`, or `Gambler`)."*
- *"Edge slots and center slots swap multipliers every `30s`."*
- *"Pin Tweaker bias range expands from `30%–70%` to `15%–85%`."*

### Dynamic "Hot Pin" Migration
Every `60s`, a glowing **Spotlight** shifts to a new 3-pin cluster, granting `+50%` modifier proc chance and `+1` base pin value. This rewards players who actively adjust **Batch Splitter** timing or **Pin Tweaker** angles mid-run rather than leaving the board static.

### "Rested & Ready" Variety Bonus
Rather than nerfing a player's favorite build, any ball type or Pin Modifier **not used** in your previous Ascension starts the next run with a **Rested & Ready** badge (`+25%` special proc chance and `-15%` cooldown for the first `5` minutes), naturally nudging players to rotate archetypes.
