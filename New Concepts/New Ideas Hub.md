#BouncyBally #NewConcepts #MOC

Central Map of Content (MOC) for new *Bouncy Bally* gameplay concepts—focused on **player agency, tactile physics, emergent synergies, and replayable modes** rather than passive `+10%` stat-scaling.

> [!important] Core Philosophy
> *Bouncy Bally* already has a strong incremental backbone in [[Bouncy Bally]] and [[Skill tree]]. These new concept notes explore how to make **every drop feel interactive, surprising, and replayable**—whether playing actively with skill shots or tuning wild automated synergies.

# How New Concepts Connect to Existing Systems

- **[[Bouncy Bally]]**: Expands the core Galton loop (`Drop → Pin Contacts → Slot Payout`) with active player interventions (aim/spin, nudges, flippers, portals) that feed directly into `AccumulatedPinBonus` and `SlotMultiplier`.
- **[[Skill tree]]**: Provides new active-play and board-element branches that complement existing [[Board upgrades]], [[Ball upgrades]], [[Pin upgrades]], and [[Lootboxes upgrades]].
- **[[Ball types]] & Batch Splitting**: Mid-air **Billiard Collisions** and cross-ball reactions give players a compelling reason to pair specific balls in **Batch Splitter** slots beyond simple cooldown averaging.
- **[[Pin upgrades|Pin Modifiers]] & Directional Tweakers**: Dynamic board zones (Wind Tunnels, Fever Spotlights, Portal Tubes) make directional tuning (`30%`–`70%`) a spatial routing puzzle rather than a static math optimization.
- **[[Plans]] & [[Prestige|Prestige Ascension]]**: New challenge modes and milestone bosses create fresh goals and build variety across Prestige runs.

> [!tip] Avoiding Overlap
> All ideas in `Ideas/New Concepts/` are designed to **complement—not duplicate—**existing mechanics (such as *Overclock*, *Dupliball*, *Bouncy rage*, *Walls*, *Bank mode*, and the 10 existing Pin Modifiers).
