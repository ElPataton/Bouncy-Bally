#SkillTree

A zoomable, draggable webbed Skill Tree where the player spends run money to unlock board capacity, ball types, pin tools, and passive multipliers. On [[Prestige|Prestige Ascension]], Skill Tree levels reset while Prestige Shards and permanent Prestige Upgrades persist.

Upgrade canvas: [[Upgrades.canvas]]

# Upgrade Categories
- [[Board upgrades]] — Batch slots (`Batch 2`–`4`), **Batch Splitter** (1–4), **Ball Cooldown** & **Rapid Recharge** (cooldown reduction), **Fortune Core** (Luck), `Pin mult`, `Double slots`, `Workshop`, and `Pin Tweaker`.
- [[Ball upgrades]] — Ball unlocks (`Basket`, `Beach`, `Ghost`, `Steel`, `Trick`, `Sticky`, `Chemical`, `Marble`, `Golden`, `Constructor`, `Gambler`) plus per-ball quantity, value, speed, cooldown, and multiplier upgrades.
- [[Pin upgrades]] — Pin Workshop access, Bulk Pin Upgrading (`UPGRADE x5`, `Row`, `Max All`, `Hold-to-Upgrade`), `Pin Tweaker`, and all 10 Pin Modifiers (`Amplifier`, `Lucky`, `Echo`, `Streak`, `Zigzag`, `Chain`, `Midas`, `Prism`, `Turbo`, `Tesla`).
- [[Lootboxes upgrades]] — Lootbox Capsules (`Starter`, `Tinker`, `Charged`) unlocked alongside the Workshop, weighted draws without replacement, and animated capsule reveals.
- [[Effects upgrades]] — Special trigger and ball-effect concepts (`Overclock`, `Dupliball`, `Bouncy rage`).
- [[Prestige]] — Meta-progression Ascension system unlocked at `$2,500` run earnings.
